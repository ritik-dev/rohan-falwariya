## What this means for the collage

Three of the five usable logos are **monochrome wordmarks** (Taos near-black,
FabuLove black script, Southwind navy script) and Dose is a single dark sage.
That suits the editorial direction: set them all in one ink colour on the bone
paper and they read as a considered client wall rather than a sponsor banner.

Colour-wise most of them already agree with the site's burnt sienna `#A73D18`:
Taos' terracotta `#C46142` and FabuLove's rose `#A02447` are the same family, and
Serenity's earthy tans are near-neighbours of the existing `--ink-3` `#6B6252`.
Southwind's navy `#093165` is the one true outlier.

Two blockers before any of this ships:

1. **Three brands can't be identified at all** (Motion, Royal Canadian, Cloudly).
   A logo wall with five of eight looks broken; either Rohan supplies those, or
   the collage stays typographic for everyone.
2. **Southwind's logo has a baked-in white background** — it needs cutting out,
   or it will sit as a white rectangle on the bone paper.

Using client logos to say who you have worked for is normal practice, but these
are third-party trademarks: only publish the ones Rohan confirms.
